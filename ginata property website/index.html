Who is Ginata made for?

Ginata is built for hosts managing short stays, holiday homes and serviced apartments. Whether you manage one property or several, it brings everyday reservations, guest details, payments and property expenses into one workspace.

Can I manage direct and Airbnb bookings?

Yes. You can record bookings from different sources, including direct, phone and Airbnb stays. Ginata supports iCal calendar import and export for supported channels. iCal shares availability dates; it is not an instant, full reservation or messaging integration.

Does Ginata process M-Pesa payments?

Ginata lets you record payments received through M-Pesa, bank transfer, cash or card, attach the actual transaction reference and track the remaining balance. Recording a payment is not the same as collecting it through a payment gateway. Host checks are labeled as manual verification.

Can I download my invoices and receipts?

Yes. You can create invoices and receipts, choose document layouts, preview PDFs with zoom controls and download them to your device. Your documents include the relevant host, guest, payment and booking details.

How does my host account work?

You have your own host account and account-based workspace. The native app uses secure device storage for session credentials, and signing out revokes that device's session. Email confirmation is available when enabled on the hosted service. Always use HTTPS and sign out on shared devices.

Where can I use Ginata?

Sign in or create an account in the Ginata web app straight from this website, or download the Android app from the download page. Both use the same host account and the same features. The Android build is a preview release that you install directly, not yet a Play Store listing.

How do I get help?

Email the Ginata team at ginata.app@gmail.com. Please never send passwords, ID documents or payment details by email.
