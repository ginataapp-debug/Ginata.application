Download the Ginata mobile app and sign in with the same host account you use on the web. Your properties, bookings, guests, payments and invoices stay in sync because both apps use the same secure Ginata service.

  1. Download the Android app (APK) on your phone.
  2. Open the file and, if Android asks, allow installs from your browser for this download.
  3. Sign in or create your host account. That's it.
